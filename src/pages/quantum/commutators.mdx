# Commutators

The commutator of two operators is defined as

$$
[A,B] = AB - BA
$$

For position and momentum,

$$
[x,p] = i\hbar
$$

If two operators commute, then

$$
[A,B] = 0
$$

This often means they can be simultaneously diagonalized in an appropriate setting.
